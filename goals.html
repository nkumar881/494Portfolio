Career Goals

Building reliable computing systems

My goal is to build a career developing reliable computing systems that connect hardware and software. I am particularly interested in embedded systems, computer architecture, firmware, and hardware verification, where understanding low-level behavior is essential to producing dependable designs.

My research and project experiences have strengthened my interest in checking whether systems behave as intended. Processor design has given me experience with datapaths, control logic, and simulation, while formal verification research has introduced me to assertions and methods for reasoning about hardware correctness.

In the near term, I want to deepen my technical skills through research, challenging coursework, and engineering experience. I also plan to pursue a master’s degree to develop greater depth in my area of specialization.

Over time, I hope to contribute to engineering teams that build useful, reliable technology. I want to continue developing my ability to communicate technical decisions, learn unfamiliar systems, and take responsibility for the quality of my work.
